GENERAL HEALTH

General health refers to the overall physical and mental well-being of a person. Maintaining good health involves balanced nutrition, regular physical activity, adequate sleep, hydration, stress management, personal hygiene, and preventive healthcare.

A healthy lifestyle can reduce the risk of several chronic health problems. Regular health checkups can help identify potential problems early.

Health information should be considered educational. Individual health concerns should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.


NUTRITION

A balanced diet provides the body with carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, fiber, and water.

Common nutritious foods include vegetables, fruits, whole grains, legumes, nuts, seeds, dairy or suitable alternatives, eggs, and other protein-rich foods.

A healthy breakfast may include foods such as oats, fruits, eggs, yogurt, whole grains, vegetables, or other balanced combinations.

It is generally useful to include a variety of foods rather than relying on one food every day.

Highly processed foods that are high in added sugars, sodium, or unhealthy fats should generally be limited as part of a balanced eating pattern.

Individual nutritional requirements can vary depending on age, activity level, health conditions, allergies, and other factors.


HYDRATION

Water is important for many functions of the human body, including temperature regulation and normal physical processes.

Fluid requirements vary between individuals and can increase with exercise, hot weather, fever, vomiting, or diarrhea.

Signs that may occur with dehydration include thirst, dry mouth, dark-colored urine, tiredness, dizziness, and reduced urination.

Severe dehydration can be dangerous and may require medical attention.


FITNESS AND EXERCISE

Regular physical activity can support cardiovascular health, muscular strength, mobility, fitness, and overall well-being.

Common forms of physical activity include walking, running, cycling, swimming, resistance training, stretching, and recreational activities.

People who are new to exercise should generally begin gradually and increase activity according to their fitness level.

A proper warm-up and appropriate exercise technique can help reduce the risk of injury.

People with certain medical conditions or concerns should consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning a new exercise program.


SLEEP

Sleep is important for physical recovery, cognitive function, mood, and general health.

Adults commonly need around 7 to 9 hours of sleep per night, although individual needs can vary.

Helpful sleep habits may include maintaining a consistent sleep schedule, keeping the bedroom comfortable and dark, limiting excessive screen exposure before bedtime, and avoiding large meals close to bedtime.

Persistent difficulty sleeping or significant daytime sleepiness should be discussed with a healthcare professional.


COMMON COLD

The common cold is a viral infection that can cause symptoms such as a runny nose, blocked nose, sore throat, coughing, sneezing, and mild fatigue.

Most common colds improve with rest, adequate fluids, and supportive care.

Antibiotics do not treat viral infections such as the common cold.

Symptoms that are severe, unusual, worsening, or persistent should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.


FEVER

Fever refers to an increase in body temperature that can occur when the body responds to an infection or another condition.

Fever may occur together with symptoms such as chills, sweating, weakness, headache, or body aches.

The importance of a fever depends on factors such as age, temperature, duration, associated symptoms, and underlying health conditions.

Very high fever, persistent fever, confusion, difficulty breathing, severe weakness, seizures, or other concerning symptoms require prompt medical evaluation.


HEADACHE

Headaches can have many possible causes, including stress, dehydration, lack of sleep, illness, eye strain, or migraine.

Rest, hydration, regular meals, and appropriate sleep may help with some mild headaches.

A sudden and extremely severe headache, especially when accompanied by weakness, confusion, difficulty speaking, loss of consciousness, or other neurological symptoms, requires urgent medical evaluation.


STOMACH PROBLEMS

Stomach discomfort can have many possible causes, including indigestion, infections, food-related problems, stress, and other medical conditions.

Common symptoms may include abdominal discomfort, nausea, vomiting, bloating, or diarrhea.

Maintaining hydration is particularly important when vomiting or diarrhea occurs.

Severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, blood in vomit or stool, fainting, severe dehydration, or worsening symptoms require medical attention.


MENTAL WELLNESS

Mental wellness is an important part of overall health.

Healthy habits that may support mental well-being include regular physical activity, adequate sleep, social connection, relaxation activities, healthy eating, and managing stress.

Feeling stressed or worried from time to time is common. Persistent or severe emotional difficulties can interfere with daily life and should be discussed with a qualified mental health professional.


HEART HEALTH

Heart health can be supported by regular physical activity, a balanced diet, maintaining a healthy weight, avoiding tobacco, managing stress, and controlling risk factors such as high blood pressure and high cholesterol.

Chest pain or pressure, severe difficulty breathing, sudden weakness, fainting, or other symptoms that could indicate a medical emergency require immediate professional medical attention.


EMERGENCY SAFETY

Some symptoms require urgent medical attention.

Examples can include severe difficulty breathing, severe chest pain or pressure, sudden loss of consciousness, seizure, severe bleeding, sudden weakness on one side of the body, sudden difficulty speaking, severe allergic reaction, or other rapidly worsening serious symptoms.

HealthAI should not attempt to diagnose an emergency. When potentially serious symptoms are described, the user should be advised to seek appropriate emergency medical care.
